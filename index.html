Civic Tool Florida Amendment 3 Impact

A responsible vote evaluates policy not simply by individual pocketbook math, but by the structural consequences on our neighbors, essential public workers, and community fabric. An $8.4 billion annual reduction in municipal ad valorem collections forces a dramatic mechanical wealth redistribution:

Renters Net Loss
  • $0 Homestead Relief: Tenants receive zero direct benefit from the $250k exemption.
  • Passed-Through Millage: Landlords pass municipal tax hikes straight to annual leases.
Estimated Impact: -$800 to -$2,000 / year
New Buyers (Lockout) Tax Penalty
  • 5-Year Lockout: Buyers entering after Dec 31, 2026, receive only the baseline $50,000 exemption.
  • Two-Tiered Citizenry: Pays thousands more than neighbors in identical homes.
Carrying Penalty: +$1,800 to +$2,400 / year
Tenured Owners Short-Term
  • Paper Cut: Initial gross reduction on non-school property taxes.
  • Clawed Back: Much of the cut is eaten by new fire assessments and utility fees.
Realistic Net: +$0 to +$650 / year
Commercial / Landlords Big Winner
  • 5% Assessment Cap: Slashes commercial assessment increase cap from 10% down to 5%.
  • Shielded Equity: Protects multi-family and retail portfolios while cities raise local fees.
Value Shielded: Tens of thousands
Erosion of Local Home Rule: When cities face structural deficits, they become financially subordinate to state legislative grants in Tallahassee, jeopardizing local parks, canal clearing, and community safety programs.
