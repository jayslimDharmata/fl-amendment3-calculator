Financial Reality Breakdown:

Because you reside in an unincorporated area like Arden, you do not pay city taxes. Your non-school levy is strictly ~8.65 mills. Expanding the exemption gives you an initial +$1,730 / year gross cut. However, when Palm Beach County converts fire rescue to a dedicated -$750 / year special assessment and adds -$350 / year in drainage/utility surcharges, your net benefit narrows to roughly +$630 / year.

Secondary Community Impacts & Long-Term Risks

Resale Liquidity & Buyer Drag

The 5-year residency lock-out imposes an extra $1,800 to $2,400 annual carrying cost on out-of-state and new buyers. In master-planned communities like Arden that rely on relocating buyers, this suppresses purchasing power and slows long-term equity growth.

Insurance Underwriting & Response Times

Squeezing municipal budgets forces freezes on fire engine and ladder truck replacements. If local ISO fire ratings slip, private property insurance premiums can spike by hundreds of dollars, wiping out annual property tax savings.

Western Corridor Road & Canal Maintenance

While internal Arden amenities are maintained by your HOA, county roads (Southern Blvd, Seminole Pratt Whitney) and regional drainage canals depend on county capital funds. Slashing ad valorem budgets stalls road expansions and canal clearing.

Untouched School Millage

The amendment provides $0 in school district relief. On a $750k home, you will continue to pay thousands annually to the Palm Beach County School Board, preserving essential school operational funding.
