Tailored Secondary Impact & Risk Analysis

Real-world consequences for your specific property tier, equity liquidity, and local services:

Future Resale Value & Buyer Liquidity

When you decide to sell your $750,000 home, out-of-state transplants and incoming buyers will be locked into the 5-year penalty tier, paying up to $2,400/year more in taxes than current residents. This suppresses purchasing power and artificially cools demand for higher-tier homes.

Emergency Response & Insurance Underwriting

Local fire-rescue capital budgets will face replacement freezes for $1.5M+ ladder apparatus and advanced rescue gear. If station staffing or equipment delays cause the local ISO Public Protection Classification to slip, private home insurance carriers will hike annual underwriting premiums by hundreds of dollars.

Infrastructure & Community Curb Appeal

Because Amendment 3 restricts ad valorem dollars solely to core mandatory services, municipal road repaving schedules, canal/stormwater drainage maintenance, and local park upkeep will face chronic delays unless offset by separate special tax referendums.

Erosion of Municipal Home Rule

Facing multi-million dollar operating gaps, local city councils and county commissions will lose financial independence, becoming client entities dependent on Tallahassee legislative grants with strict political strings attached.
